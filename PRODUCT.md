# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS, no build step, deployed on Netlify (confirmed by Mostafa: "use the same").

## Users

People who come across Mostafa through his teaching and his content: students and others learning AI and cloud. They arrive from a class, a LinkedIn post or a shared link, usually on a phone, and want to know who he is and where to find his work.

## Product Purpose

A personal hub for Mostafa Saher, in the manner of nicksaraev.com and nateherk.com: say who he is, link to his work everywhere, and collect newsletter sign-ups. Success is an email sign-up.

## Positioning

A working technical instructor who explains AI and cloud the way he does in class: one clear step at a time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Newsletter sign-up is the primary action. The newsletter has no name for now; "After Class" is retired. No email provider is chosen yet, so sign-ups are captured with Netlify Forms.
- The magazine concept and its design are retired.
- Undecided: newsletter name, email provider, employer or institution to name, links to video and social channels.

## Brand Commitments

- Reference sites named by Mostafa as the model: nicksaraev.com and nateherk.com.
- Plain, direct, first-person voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portrait cut-out: `v2/drafts/assets/mostafa-cutout.png`.
- Biography facts: technical instructor in AI and cloud; computer engineer; BSc Computer Engineering with a Computer Science minor, American University of Sharjah, 2020; summer internship at an indie game studio in Abu Dhabi.
- Projects with public repositories: Arab COVID-19 Map, ASIM, Maze Runner, LabServe.
- Links: LinkedIn, GitHub, email.
- Absent, never to be invented: subscriber or student counts, employer names, certifications, testimonials, press, video or social links beyond the above.

## Product Principles

1. The sign-up comes first and is never more than one scroll away.
2. Link out to the work; the site is a hub, not the destination.
3. Only real facts. A missing number is left out, not estimated.
4. Explain plainly, as in class.
