About

I teach AI and cloud for a living. The best part of every class comes once it’s over: the questions in the corridor, the “wait, so why does that work?”, the links I send people afterwards.

The newsletter is where that goes now. Each one has my thoughts from teaching, an honest opinion on the latest models and cloud services, and resources I have tested before sharing.

Before teaching I trained as a computer engineer. I graduated from the American University of Sharjah in 2020 with a major in computer engineering and a minor in computer science, and spent a summer building games at an indie studio in Abu Dhabi.
