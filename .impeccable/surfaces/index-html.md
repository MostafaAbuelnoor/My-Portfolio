# Home (index.html)

## Scope and mode
The whole soft-launch site: one page. Mode: Persuade. Success = a learner leaves their email.

## Audience, job, action
Students and ICs studying generative AI on AWS and Claude, often at night after work. They should understand in seconds who Mostafa is and what he teaches, and subscribe for the free Claude Foundation practice exam, which is not written yet: promise it as coming, never link it.

## Proof and content
Real: portrait (LinkedIn Photo_black.png), AWS Certified Instructor, newsletter topics. One sample question is authored demonstration content, labelled as a sample; Mostafa verifies it. No counts, logos or testimonials.

## Constraints
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build. Form works on day one through Netlify Forms; one constant switches it to the newsletter service's endpoint later. Dark only, per the brief.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a certification exam screen. Scrolling moves through questions, the last one asks where to send your free practice exam, and your email is the answer. Refuses the creator hub: a round portrait, "Hey friends", a newsletter card.

OWN-WORLD: Night-mode test console. Graphite ground, black exhibit pane, flat amber for the offer and flags, flat green for answered and correct, red only for a wrong answer. Hairline panes and option rows with letter keys (A–D), a numbered review grid, and a monospaced elapsed timer. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything, with Mono for timer, counters and data. No glow, no gradients, no cards. The MS mark is built from review-grid cells.

STORY: Visitors meet Mostafa as the exam author, try one real sample question and see the explanation, then subscribe to reserve the practice exam. Success shows as a score report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Exam bar across the top: the mark and name on the left, "Question 1 of 3" in the centre, the timer on the right. Below it the split item layout: a black exhibit pane on the left holds the portrait full height, and the right pane holds a huge "Hello, I'm Mostafa.", one sentence on what he teaches, a four-option question ("What are you preparing for?") and the amber action to get the free practice exam. A sticky bottom bar holds Previous, the review grid (1 2 3) and Next.

FORM: Exam Console, #1 on my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the roll). Seed key a0aedf54. Raises kept from declined challengers: the logo mark is built on the review grid (Crouwel), and colour prints flat at full saturation (Angura).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
