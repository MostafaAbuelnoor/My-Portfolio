# Hub homepage (v2/drafts/hub.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: learners who met Mostafa through teaching or content, mostly on phones. Action: subscribe by email. Proof: his own biography, four public projects, links out. Unresolved: newsletter name, email provider, video and social links.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A creator hub that puts the sign-up inside the first viewport and then gets out of the way as a plain index of links. It refuses the magazine cover and the stat-counter hero he has no numbers for.

OWN-WORLD: Highlighter on a white page. A drenched marker-yellow hero field, near-black ink, a white marker swipe behind the key phrase, his black-jacket cut-out standing on the yellow. Below it, white paper with hairline-ruled link rows. Bricolage Grotesque headings, Schibsted Grotesk text. Pills only for controls.

STORY: He teaches AI and cloud clearly; I can get his notes by email; here is everything else he has made.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Yellow field edge to edge. Nav on the field (name left, three links, black Subscribe pill). Left: headline "I teach AI and cloud, one clear step at a time." at display scale with the white swipe behind "one clear step", one sentence of who he is, then the email field and Subscribe button in one white pill. Right: the portrait, anchored to the hero's bottom edge. On phones the copy and form come first, portrait below.

FORM: The two sites Mostafa named, combined: Nate Herk's hero (claim, portrait, action) over Nick Saraev's index (bio, ruled link lists, newsletter close). seed: none (pinned) — "something similar to https://nicksaraev.com/ and https://www.nateherk.com/ where he links to his work everywhere". Signature interaction: the subscribe control, which presses, shows progress in place and resolves to a confirmation without a page change. Motion: one staggered hero entrance and the swipe drawing in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
