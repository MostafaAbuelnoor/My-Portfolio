WB. Issue One, unslopped Newest

Same cover, six colours, layout and photo as WA. Quieter type and much calmer motion.

Type
Headings at weight 500 instead of 700–800 (the logo and stamp at 600). Ten text sizes instead of twelve. No periods on headlines.
Shapes and spacing
The email field and buttons have 10px corners (6px inside the field) instead of full pills, and the field border is a soft line instead of solid black. All spacing on a 4px grid. Thinner arrow and check icons.
Motion
One easing curve. One short fade-in for the cover on load. Nothing moves on scroll. No bounce on the check mark or the card, no ripple through the name, no swell after load, no page corner, and no shake on a wrong address (the red border and message stay). The subscriber label now fades in, and the stamp fades on instead of being pressed.
Kept
The name still gets a little bolder near the cursor, because it changes weight rather than moving.
